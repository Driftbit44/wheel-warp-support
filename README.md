# Wheel Warp support

Pick a world. Your legs are the engine.

Wheel Warp is an indoor cycling arcade for iPhone and iPad. A compatible Bluetooth
FTMS or Cycling Power trainer powers the games. Controllers and heart-rate straps
are optional accessories. You can preview games without a trainer in demo mode.

## Get help

[Ask a question or report a problem](https://github.com/Driftbit44/wheel-warp-support/issues/new).
Include your device model, iOS version, app version, accessory model, and steps to
reproduce the issue. Issues are public: please do not post passwords, personal,
workout, or heart-rate data.

## Connection help

- Allow Bluetooth in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Bluetooth → Wheel Warp.
- Wake the trainer by pedalling. Close other training apps that may hold its connection.
- Use the device status controls or Settings in Wheel Warp to run setup again.
- Pair a PS5 DualSense in iOS Bluetooth settings. Pair the right-hand Zwift Click v2
  puck and Bluetooth heart-rate straps inside Wheel Warp.
- When stopping a ride, use Pause or exit the game to release trainer resistance.

Your scoreboard and settings are stored on your device. Keep the app installed to
retain them across app updates. Deleting the app removes local app data.
