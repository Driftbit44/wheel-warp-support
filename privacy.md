# Wheel Warp privacy policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026.

Wheel Warp processes cycling trainer readings, optional heart-rate readings, controller
input, and rider settings on your device to run its games. Rider settings can include
FTP and weight. Bluetooth device names and identifiers are saved locally to help
reconnect your accessories. Completed ride results, including optional average and
maximum heart rate, are saved to a local scoreboard.

The app does not send this information to the developer or to an analytics or
advertising service. It has no account system, ads, or tracking SDK. It does not
access Apple Health or use HealthKit.

Bluetooth permission is used to discover and communicate with your trainer,
controller, and heart-rate strap. You can withdraw permission in iOS Settings.

Your local records remain on your device until deleted. You can delete individual
scoreboard entries in the app. Deleting the app removes its local app data; iOS backup
and restore may retain or restore data according to your Apple settings. The developer
does not receive your iOS backups.

If you contact support, the information you choose to send is used to respond to your
request. Avoid sending workout or heart-rate information unless you want to share it.
Apple separately handles App Store purchases, distribution, and any diagnostic sharing
you enable under its own privacy policy.

For support or privacy questions, open an issue at
https://github.com/Driftbit44/wheel-warp-support/issues. GitHub issues are public;
do not include personal, health, or other sensitive information. GitHub processes
information you submit to its website under its own privacy policy.
